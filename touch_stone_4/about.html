About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: good food can turn an ordinary moment into a reason to gather. We wanted to create a welcoming place for Kirkland neighbors, where picking up a loaf of bread or choosing a few cookies could become a familiar part of the day.

That idea guides the way we bake. We give our slow-fermented breads time to develop their flavor, shape each loaf by hand, and make our cookies in small batches. Our menu focuses on these two everyday favorites so we can give each batch the care it deserves.

We picture our breads at family dinners and our cookies shared over coffee, tucked into lunch bags, or brought along when visiting a friend. Whether you stop in for something for yourself or place a pre order to share, we hope North Star Bakery becomes a small, welcoming part of your neighborhood routine.

Sourcing philosophy

We choose quality ingredients, seasonal and local when available.
